# Manufacturing design policy — MI-036
Status: active design constraints, not validated performance requirements.
User constraint: no in-house production equipment; standard purchased components and outsourced fabrication are permitted. Optimize economic manufacturing cost per conforming unit, not minimum component price. Prototype budget around JPY 10,000 was discussed previously as a partial BOM, not a hard all-in limit.
## Mandatory design reviews
- No machining, drilling, tapping, welding, custom crimping or press fitting required by assembler. Purchase finished parts and preterminated cables; outsource any necessary fabrication. Basic screwdriver and USB PC permitted as planning assumption; verify with user if additional tools needed.
- Prefer off-the-shelf enclosure with pre-cut openings or outsourced finished panels. No dependence on owning a 3D printer.
- Use common datum and self-locating interfaces, fixed stops, keyed connectors, captive/standard fasteners; no routine shim, hand bending, trimming or manual potentiometer tuning.
- Keep conductive/ferromagnetic fasteners, frames and PCB copper out of sensitive coil/weak magnetic measurement regions unless characterized. Use appropriate polymer fixtures and independent support for hydrostatic weighing; evaluate creep, stiffness, water/heat resistance. Separate wet zone and electronics.
- Separate measurement cartridge(s) and shared electronics; do not require all experimental principles in one head. Design strain relief, polarity protection, current/temperature limits, magnet retention and safe travel.
- Use preassembled electronics first; move to outsourced PCBA once circuit and test interface stable. Keep vendor-neutral CAD, Gerber, BOM, placement files, drawings and assembly instructions under version control; no proprietary user data in public repository.
- Firmware stores raw signals, calibration revision, serial number and test outcomes; fixture acceptance via software, not subjective operator tuning. Calibrate per unit if needed; reject/repair units that fail rather than hiding error.
## Targets to verify (not achieved)
Assembly tools <=2 common hand tools; unique screw sizes <=2; manually soldered joints 0 in production; manual adjustments 0; assembly <=20 min and calibration/test <=15 min for mature PCBA version; fixture replacement without recalibration only if interchangeability demonstrated; first-pass yield >=95% pilot target. Track part count, connector count, unique fasteners, vendor count, setup time, actual assembly/test time, first-pass yield, final yield, rework and field return rates. 
## Design gates
G0: prove physics with modules and purchased fixtures; G1: obtain actual supplier quotations and build assembly drawing, tolerance budget and test procedure; G2: 3–5 pilot units by independent assembler with timing and interchangeability checks; G3: 10–20 unit outsourced PCBA pilot after circuit validation, with electrical/thermal/magnetic performance acceptance and supplier quality checks. No production release until pass. Quote 1/5/10/20/50/100 units incl logistics, tax if applicable, minimum order and lead time. Include engineering, tooling, certification, packaging, returns and inventory costs separately in commercial model.
