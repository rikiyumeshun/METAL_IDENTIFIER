# MI-034 — Adaptive measurement selection
## Method
27 grades and listed states from v0.1. Equal grade priors, uniform state priors within grade. Discretize log density/conductivity/thermal into bins approximately one combined standard deviation wide, with tail bins; magnetic binary observation error 2%. Model assumes independence conditional on listed state. At each step compute expected reduction of grade entropy across candidate measurement bins, divided by assumed measurement time. Compare full measurement, fixed order magnet/conductivity/density/thermal with early stop, and adaptive order with early stop. Stop when max nominal posterior >=.8/.9/.95 or all tests consumed; otherwise abstain. Top1 at stop counts all samples including abstentions, ties fractionally scored. No unknown-class rejection.
## Simulation
180 samples per grade, 4860 shared synthetic draws. Three data-generating scenarios: independent matched, conductivity/thermal material correlation .65, and same correlation plus fixed log biases .015/.08/-.08. Within-state material log SD .005/.07/.07, instrument SD .01/.05/.10. Model ignores correlation and bias intentionally. Times density 15 s, conductivity 3 s, thermal 12 s, magnet 2 s are hypothetical; exclude setup and calibration. 27 reported conditions, 131220 condition evaluations, not independent specimens.
At correlated scenario and threshold .90: all 32 s, 36.91% coverage, 97.38% accepted accuracy, 72.51% top1; fixed 27.31 s, 37.39%, 96.64%, 72.37%; adaptive 25.48 s, 37.67%, 96.50%, 72.37%. Adaptive time saving 20.36% vs all and 6.70% vs fixed early stop. At correlation+bias, accepted accuracy at threshold .90 falls to 94.52% all and 90.76% adaptive. At threshold .95 adaptive 94.87% accepted accuracy, 32.08% coverage, 26.68 s. No safe grade guarantee; uncertainty is misspecified. These values depend on synthetic distributions and assumed costs. Do not extrapolate to the 152-feature registry.
