# MI-025 — Periodic thermal excitation / Angstrom method
## Physics
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10786416/ and https://doi.org/10.1007/s10765-024-03479-0 ; heat loss extension https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0017931021015659 . Long uniform rod, 1D linear heat equation with distributed linear loss, dT/dt=alpha*T_xx-beta*T. Harmonic spatial transfer between two locations separated by L: H=exp[-(u+i*v)*L], where u+iv=sqrt((beta+i*omega)/alpha). Ideal phase lag phi=vL and log attenuation A=uL. Loss-free estimate alpha=omega*L^2/(2*phi^2); joint estimate alpha=omega*L^2/(2*A*phi), exact in the assumed model even with distributed linear heat loss. The ratio cancels common excitation amplitude and phase, not individual sensor transfer functions. Alpha=k/(rho*cp); alpha alone cannot identify k and cp separately. Independent volumetric heat capacity or calibrated heat flux/effusivity is needed.
## Simulation
Six reference materials, 500 draws each, shared 3000 synthetic specimens. 2 loss ranges x 2 sensor lag ranges x 3 noise levels x 2 frequency sets x 2 estimators = 48 conditions, 144000 condition-method trials. Material log alpha SD .05 assumed. L=.01 m, near-sensor oscillation .8-2 K, distributed loss beta uniform 0-.08 s^-1 when enabled; independent sensor first-order time constants uniform 0-.5 s when enabled. Add independent complex quadrature noise SD .01/.05/.1 K per sensor per frequency (not raw thermistor noise spec). Frequencies .05 alone or .02/.05/.1/.2 Hz. Require both amplitudes >=5 noise SD, positive phase lag >.08 rad and <pi-.05, and joint attenuation >.08. Median of valid per-frequency estimates, no phase unwrapping. If none valid abstain. Nearest nominal log alpha classifier. Closed set only. Top1_all counts abstentions as wrong; top1_valid conditional on valid. No unknown rejection.
## Results at phasor noise .05 K, loss enabled, ideal sensor response
| method | frequencies | median relative alpha error | all-case top1 | coverage |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| phase | .05 |20.95%|43.17%|62.53%|
| joint | .05 |11.99%|48.70%|62.53%|
| phase | four |21.09%|53.37%|89.70%|
| joint | four |8.72%|66.83%|89.70%|
With lag enabled, four-frequency joint median error 12.07%, top1 62.70%, coverage 89.60%. The 0.02 Hz cycle takes 50 s; several steady-state cycles may require minutes. No time-domain heater dynamics, finite-end reflections, radial gradients, contact errors beyond lag, frequency-specific heat loss, or transient start-up included. Therefore not a fast handheld measurement guarantee.
