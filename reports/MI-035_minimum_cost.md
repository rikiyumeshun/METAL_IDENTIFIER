# MI-035 — Minimum marginal-cost prototype
## Strategy
Start from available smartphone/PC, small magnet, water and scale; do not buy a complete automated instrument before proving information value. Camera color and microphone modes are exploratory only (MI-026/028). Magnet pendulum damping is an alternative conductivity transducer, not independent information; validate friction, geometry, thickness, baseline, and repeatability before assigning any conductivity accuracy. Never put strong magnets near the phone or unsecured ferromagnetic objects. No destructive chemistry or high-current improvised mains supply.

Excludes shipping, tools, calibration standards and sample procurement. No actual purchases.
## Numerical screening
27 grades, 500 samples per grade, same 13500 synthetic draws reused for 3 sample masses x 3 weighing SD x 3 conductivity log noise x 5 measurement protocols = 1,822,500 condition evaluations. Nominal v0.1 data, assumed scatter, equal grade priors and equal state priors. Density log SD approximated hypot(.005, weighing_SD*hypot(1-rho,rho)/mass) with independent air/submerged mass readings, water density approx 1 g/cm3; normal log approximation increasingly weak at large noise/small buoyancy. Conductivity log SD hypot(.07,readout SD .05/.15/.30); thermal log SD hypot(.07,.15), material correlation .65 in generation, ignored by classifier; magnetic binary flip 2%. No unknown classes, actual calibration drift, buoyancy fixture errors, optical/acoustic signatures or empirical data. Families Al, Cu (includes brass/bronze), Ti, steel including stainless. Family prediction sums grade likelihoods; grade ties fractional. Family priors reflect equal grade priors, not equal family priors.
100g, weighing SD .1g, conductivity log readout SD .15: magnet alone 7.26% grade and 65.48% family; density+magnet 44.01%/99.83%; conductivity+magnet 39.76%/88.76%; density+conductivity+magnet 61.99%/100%; all four 68.86%/100%. Density+magnet grade top1 drops to 24.61% at 20g; improves to 53.17% at 100g/.01g. Density+conductivity+magnet coverage at posterior .90 is 17.4%, accepted accuracy 98.72%; closed-set nominal confidence is not a grade assurance. A .1g weighing SD is not equivalent to .1g display resolution. 
## Pilot
A: repeated air mass and displaced-water readings of documented coupons, nonporous samples only. Suspend from independent support and keep off vessel; tare hanger, remove bubbles, record water temperature. A scale under water vessel may measure displaced water directly; its covariance differs from simulated air/submerged protocol and needs separate validation. B: magnet pass/fail. C: use existing phone recording for controlled coupon geometry; test pendulum ringdown with no specimen and reference specimens, fixed gap, safe small magnet, repeat placement. D: if need electrical acquisition, use shared slow ADC and Kelvin contacts on known cross-section bars, stable current-limited low-voltage source, shunt calibration and input common-mode validation. Do not promise .15 log conductivity noise from BOM; demonstrate it. Then consider thermopower and weak susceptibility after baseline validation. All raw traces/failed readings recorded by lot.
