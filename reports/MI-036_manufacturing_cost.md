## Cost model
Expected economic cost per good unit = (recurring material, outsourced mechanics, logistics + hourly labor rate*(assembly+calibration/test+rework probability*rework minutes)/60)/final yield + fixed setup/good units. Simplified expected value, constant rates at each quantity; no inventory, tax accounting, packaging, certification, warranty or general design R&D included. Rework assumed reflected in final yield, failed attempts incur full cost. All inputs hypothetical planning allowances, not quotes; same baseline functional scope assumed, equivalence not established. Fixed 0/12000/65000 JPY are illustrative incremental implementation/setup allowances, not NRE quotations. Owner time valued even if not paid. 
54 scenarios: 3 architectures, 6 batch sizes, 3 labor rates. At 3000 JPY/h: handwired 21316 per good unit; connectorized 28071 at 1, 18471 at 5, 16191 at 100; outsourced PCBA 75798 at 1, 17298 at 10, 14048 at 20, 11448 at 100. Scenario crossover connectorized vs handwired around 3 good units; PCBA vs connectorized around 11 good units. Not a procurement rule; sensitivity and actual quotations required. Cash-only values available separately; both metrics should be tracked.
## Architecture decision
First prototype: prebuilt modules and standard/prefabricated enclosure; separate common controller and removable heads for DC/thermopower, induction, hydrostatic/weak magnetic force. Avoid a universal fixture that compromises all modalities. Use same connector family where electrically appropriate but key incompatible ports. Use removable nonconductive/nonmagnetic spacers close to coil; isolate wet and heated test stations. No custom machined precision fit unless tolerance analysis proves needed. Prefer reference-based calibration where stable; characterize mechanical repeatability and temperature effects. No guarantee of identical accuracy between configurations.
