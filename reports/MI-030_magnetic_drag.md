# MI-030 — Magnetic drag as a mechanical conductivity transducer
## Evidence and model
IBM published patent application US20130342191, equations 1 and 7: https://patents.justia.com/patent/20130342191 . Linear motion of a magnetic dipole oriented normal to a thin, laterally large nonmagnetic conducting sheet; low-speed/quasistatic approximation. F=3*mu0^2*m^2*sigma*t*v/(64*pi*d^4). d is dipole center-to-sheet distance (not surface gap); magnitude shown. Conductivity and thickness are inseparable unless thickness known. The patent also proposes multi-distance measurements; this is not a novel principle or patent clearance. Related https://doi.org/10.1088/0143-0807/25/5/002 and https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304885326002428 (abstract). 
Illustrative m=.2 A m², t=.5 mm, v=.2 m/s, nominal d=8 mm. At 8 mm: Cu 1.335 mN, A1050A .794, A6061-T6 .574, A5052 .467, Ti Grade 2 .0419, SUS304 .0315. Input conductivity from v0.1 nominal/proxy. Force is predicted, not measured; magnet moment not verified on a real part. At d=12 mm, force falls to (8/12)^4 ~19.75%. Low-speed condition v << 2/(mu0*sigma*t) holds for nominal inputs. Finite-size conductor, nearby edges, ferromagnetism, friction, vibrations, actuator coupling, magnet variation and actual thickness uncertainty excluded.
## Two-distance comparison
Same 6,000 synthetic coupons reused across 36 conditions (216,000 condition-method trials). Within-grade conductivity log SD .05. Unknown gap d=max(5 mm,8 mm + Gaussian error), SD .1/.5/1 mm. Independent force noise SD .01/.05/.2 mN; second distance step 2 or 4 mm exactly known. Fixed-gap estimate assumes 8 mm; dual-distance estimate d_hat=step/((F1/F2)^(1/4)-1), with rejection if either force <=3 noise SD, F1<=F2, or estimated d outside 4–16 mm. Nearest nominal conductivity in log space. No density fusion or unknown-grade rejection. 
At .5 mm gap SD, .01 mN force noise, 2 mm step: fixed gap median relative conductivity error 18.71%, coverage 99.98%, all-case correct 62.53%; dual gap 11.51%, coverage 67.50%, all-case correct 47.42%, accepted correct 70.25%. Conditional errors compare different accepted subsets; not a paired improvement claim. At .05 mN noise dual gap worsens median relative error to 46.89%, coverage 58.95%, all-case correct 20.90%. Distance correction amplifies force noise and rejection especially for low-conductivity grades. For dual-gap to be a robust low-cost modality, control distance mechanically or fit multiple distances with calibrated noise model. 
## Engineering decision
Do not count magnetic drag as an independent bulk material feature when conductivity is already measured. It is a potential alternative transducer, particularly for thin known-thickness nonmagnetic sheet. Use a stationary force sensor and guided low-speed translation with direction reversal, empty-fixture baseline, reference magnet and sheet, nonmagnetic fixture, guarded travel and pinch protection. No spinning magnet needed for initial tests. Evaluate force repeatability and magnetic attraction/cross-axis load first; no grade claim without actual validation.
