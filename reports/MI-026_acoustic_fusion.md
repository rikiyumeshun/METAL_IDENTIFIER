# MI-026 — Impulse excitation and acoustic fusion
## References
ASTM E1876-22 https://store.astm.org/standards/e1876 (dynamic moduli of suitable slender bars, rods and discs); ASTM E2001-24 https://store.astm.org/e2001-24.html (resonant ultrasound for defect detection). The present simple beam model is not an implementation of E1876, which includes finite thickness and other corrections. Nominal sources listed per grade in data/MI-026_elastic_reference.csv. Conductivity values from provisional v0.1, 5052 O/H is a proxy for H32, static nominal E is used as a proxy for dynamic E.
## Model
Free-free slender homogeneous rectangular bar: c=sqrt(E/rho), fundamental longitudinal f_l=c/(2L), first nonzero bending f_b=K*h*c/L^2 with K=4.7300407449^2/(2*pi*sqrt(12)). Known geometry allows estimation of c from either mode. Ratio f_b/f_l=2*K*h/L can independently cross-check aspect ratio, but both modes encode the same E/rho; cannot treat as independent material features. Density plus c gives E, with correlated errors. Bending-only uncorrected baseline assumes fixed nominal L=.21 m and thickness .004 m. 
7 nominal grades, 600 synthetic samples each, same 4200 draws for all nine combinations of thickness log SD .002/.01/.03 and frequency log SD .001/.005/.02; seven classifiers per combination, 264600 condition-method evaluations (not independent specimens). Length .18-.24 m, thickness .002-.006 m uniform; length measurement log SD .002, additional mode model errors .005 bending/.003 longitudinal. Within-grade assumed log SD E .02, rho .005, conductivity .07; independent latent property variations. Readout log SD density .01, conductivity .05. Nearest nominal Gaussian score, accounts for covariance between log acoustic speed and density due to material density. Equal priors. No rejection; no empirical acoustic waveform, incorrect peak picks, bandwidth, support/temperature variability or real material correlations. Assumed known mode identity. 
## Findings at frequency log SD .005 and thickness log SD .01
Conductivity 74.02%; density+conductivity 88.81%; uncorrected bending 19.38%; normalized bending 37.02%; longitudinal 39.14%; density+conductivity+bending 89.40%; density+conductivity+longitudinal 89.69%. Bending speed median relative error .866%, longitudinal .428%. At thickness SD .03, normalized bending 33.26%, fusion 88.90%, median error 2.10%. Under this restricted set the incremental material information is small even with accurate acoustic measurements. E/rho is similar across Al, stainless and Ti. This is a negative result for this specific feature, not all acoustic methods.
