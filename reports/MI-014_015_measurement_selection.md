# MI-014 / MI-015 — Targeted measurement selection
Date: 2026-11-27.

## MI-014
Seven AA grade composition ranges (2017, 2024, 5052, 6061, 6063, 7050, 7075), eight elements. Source: thyssenkrupp Materials NA aluminum chemical composition table. Single numeric entries are maxima, modeled as [0,max]; dash is missing, not zero. Japanese grade labels are shorthand; verify applicable JIS and product form before deployment. Independent rectangular bounds deliberately ignore joint chemistry constraints. Seven bounded-error scenarios and all 255 nonempty subsets screened; 21 unordered grade pairs. A pair is robustly distinguishable by an element if interval gap > 2*absolute error. This is not a classification accuracy estimate. At error ±0.02 wt percentage points Cu alone resolves 18 pairs, Cu+Mg resolves 20, Cu+Mg+Cr resolves 21. At ±0.05, optimal three resolve 20; at ±0.1, 19; ±0.25, 18; ±0.5, 17. A6061/A6063 Cu gap .05 wt percentage points, requires error strictly below .025. A7050/A7075 Cr gap .14, requires error strictly below .07. These are total bounded assay errors, including matrix and sampling bias, not detection limits. No cheap sensor has demonstrated those precisions yet. Published tables are not certified samples; do not assume all target metals are in candidate set.
Reproduce: python simulation/MI-014_element_subset.py. Results generated by JS enumeration during this run; Python equivalent not yet executed.
## MI-015
Additional nominal E, CTE, cp for nine grades; no imputation of missing cp. Sources: Alumeco and BSSA. Aluminum and stainless reference condition differences limit cross-source comparison. BSSA table is for grouped AISI/EN grades, not certified JIS values.
For a 100 mm sample with uniform 40 K rise, differential nominal expansion is |alpha1-alpha2|*0.1*40 micrometers. A5052/A5083: 0.4 um, A1050A/A5052: 0.8 um, A1050A/A2024: 1.6 um, SUS410/SUS430: 2 um, SUS304/SUS316: zero in BSSA reference. A simple 10 um dial indicator is inadequate for these close pairs. Lever amplification may improve readout but not thermal drift or material scatter. Not a blanket rejection of expansion for broad-family classification.
