## Shared slow analog front end
MCP3425 datasheet https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/OTH/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/22072b.pdf : 16-bit at 15 SPS, gain 8, input-referred LSB 7.8125 uV. Resolution is not accuracy, noise floor, or guaranteed usable sensitivity. Need differential common-mode bias within ADC input limits, protection and verified current shunt; use safe current-limited low-voltage source, switch polarity and settle. Use Kelvin contacts and symmetric +/- I: R=(Vplus-Vminus)/(2I), E=(Vplus+Vminus)/2. E includes parasitic offsets and junction thermovoltages, not automatically material-only. Take zero-current and zero-gradient reference readings; control actual contact temperature and current; check self-heating/Peltier effects and thermal drift. Slow ADC cannot digitize the proposed 300 kHz coil waveform; synchronous analog demodulation or a separate suitable fast acquisition channel required. DDS bare IC needs clock, PCB and driver. 
## Signal budgets
Rectangular homogeneous coupons, voltage contact spacing 50 mm, area 1/10/100 mm², current .1/.5/1 A; nominal conductivity v0.1. R=L/(sigma*A), V=IR. Independent hypothetical 10 uV SD per polarity gives 7.07 uV SD of half-difference, excluding systematic errors, current/geometry uncertainty and material scatter. At 10 mm², 1 A: Cu 86.2 uV, A6061-T6 200.5, A5052 246.3, S45C 833.3, SUS304 3649.6, SUS316 3759.4. Al pair difference 45.8 uV; Gaussian equal-prior 95% binary accuracy would require total per-class SD below 13.9 uV. Not empirical accuracy. Four-point surface probe on arbitrary parts cannot use simple bar formula; geometry correction required. Joule heating CSV includes sample only, not contacts/driver.
Hydrostatic rho=rho_water*m_air/(m_air-m_submerged), rho_water approximated 1 g/cm³. Independent equal weighing SD .01/.1/1 g; first-order propagated uncertainty ignores buoyancy of fixture, bubbles, water temperature, surface tension and calibration error. At 100 g and .1 g SD per weighing: relative density SD Cu 1.20%, Al 0.32%, SUS304 1.05%; at 20 g: 5.98%,1.60%,5.24%. SD is not display resolution. Small samples need better mass readout or controlled volume. No density on installed parts.
## Prototype acceptance gates
1. Shorted input and stable reference voltage: measure repeatability and drift over time and temperature; current-reversal check on known resistors and certified coupons.
2. Thermal gradients 0/10/20/30 K: fit slope/intercept; check heating/cooling repeatability. Heater needs independent overtemperature cutoff, insulated contacts and safe low-voltage source.
3. Test documented Al/Cu/steel coupons with at least three independent lots initially, varied contact pressure/geometry. Assess repeatability and between-lot variation before claiming grades.
4. Calibrate load cell at relevant mass using known weights, independently verify immersed setup. 
5. Build coil head only after validating actual impedance and demodulation. Separate DC/thermal head from inductive acquisition.
