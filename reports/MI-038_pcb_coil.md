## Calculations
45 feasible single-sided circular coils, 9 frequencies 100 Hz to 1 MHz, 405 rows. Outer diameter 6/10/16/24/32 mm, track width .2/.3/.5 mm, space .2 mm, turns 4/8/12/16/20, inner diameter >=2 mm. L=mu0*N^2*davg/2*(ln(2.46/fill)+.2*fill^2) using circular current-sheet coefficients. Copper thickness 35 um, rho 1.724e-8 ohm m; approximate trace length pi*N*davg; excludes return trace, via, lead, etch tolerance and AC losses. Qdc=omega*L/Rdc is optimistic at high frequency, resonance capacitor ideal, ignores parasitics. A 2-layer PCB with coil on one side and center return on other is candidate, not a released layout. Keep metal, planes and other coils away from active area; test mutual coupling if several on one board.
Normalized half-space spatial-mode reflection r=(q-k)/(q+k), q=sqrt(k^2+j*omega*mu0*sigma), k=2/davg chosen heuristic, nonmagnetic thick homogeneous plate, no edge; contrast=abs(r6061-r5052), 0.5 mm gap attenuation exp(-2kh). Impedance contrast proxy omega*L*attenuation*contrast assumes unit coupling, NOT actual coil impedance or full Dodd-Deeds integration. No conductivity accuracy or alloy classification claim. Nominal sigma from v0.1. Skin depth sqrt(1/(pi*f*mu0*sigma)). At 16mm/12 turns, L1.411uH R.631 ohm; at 24mm/20 turns L4.876uH R1.465 ohm. Qdc at 1kHz .014/.021, at 100kHz 1.40/2.09, at 1MHz 14.05/20.91. Relative reflection contrast for 16mm at 1kHz .0480, 100kHz .00897, 1MHz .00297. Unit coupling absolute impedance proxy increases over these frequencies but measurement noise, phase errors, parasitics and model validity determine actual SNR. Do not infer optimal frequency from either metric alone.
## Temperature budget
At 100kHz 16mm and 24mm unit-coupling contrast proxy 6.54 and 17.47 mOhm respectively. Apply hypothetical .03/.1/.3 coupling factors as sensitivity ranges only. For 24mm at .1, contrast proxy 1.747 mOhm, equivalent Rdc drift ~.303 K at copper TCR .00393/K, total equal-Gaussian binary SD target <.531 mOhm ignoring material scatter. This is magnitude comparison, not exact complex impedance temperature error. Reference coil, board temperature sensor, air baseline and complex ratio deserve evaluation. Reference coil must be isolated from sample field and thermal gradients.
## Architecture
Start with 16 and 24mm single-active-layer coils, external vendor-assembled connector and test pads; separate coil coupons for R&D to prevent coupling. Compare direct complex impedance at 1/10/100/300 kHz and LC resonance frequency at roughly 1 MHz; no LDC1612 purchase decision yet. Low-frequency Q too low for robust resonant-only approach on these coils, direct synchronous voltage/current measurement needs phase calibration. LDC1612 is inductance-to-digital, not direct complex impedance measurement. Multi-layer coils may increase inductance but mutual coupling, resistance, parasitic capacitance and thickness stack need a new model; defer until actual measurements. Compare manufactured reference coupon variants at known lift-off and thickness, then test held-out material lots. First measure Rdc, L, Q and thermal drift before any grade prediction. No in-house soldering in mature production; supplier-mounted connector and common keyed harness, polymer carrier with integral contact pads. Price includes outsourced assembly and calibration, not bare PCB promotion alone. Actual Gerbers, DRC, quote, and experiments remain undone. Public repository. Study function executed and results recorded; standalone Node script not independently run.
