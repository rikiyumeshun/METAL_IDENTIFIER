## Resonant tank gate
128 combinations, MI-038 nominal 16 and 24 mm coil L and Rdc, frequencies .5/1/2/3/4/5/8/10 MHz, hypothetical R=Rdc times 1/1.5/2/3 and L=Lair times 1/.7. Series RL in parallel with ideal capacitor: Q=omega*L/R; at zero susceptance Rp=R+(omega*L)^2/R, C=L/(R²+(omega*L)²). This is a per-operating-point retuned tank calculation; a fixed capacitor will shift frequency with target, so gates do not guarantee fixed-C operation. At 1 MHz unloaded 16 mm Rp125 ohm, 24 mm Rp642 ohm: both fail Rp gates. At 3 MHz 24 mm nominal Rp5768 ohm, stress L*.7 and R*2 gives Rp1416 ohm, Q21.96; at 4 MHz stress Rp2515 ohm. 16 mm 5 MHz under same stress Rp764 ohm. Loss multipliers hypothetical, not measured; real proximity losses, self resonance, capacitance, EMC, sensor amplitude and target loading excluded. Candidate 24 mm with 330 or 560 pF C0G/NP0 initial separate build; verify actual frequency, Q, Rp across targets and temperature before ordering integrated head; reference air channel and separate LDC1101 or LDC1612 comparison. No extrapolation of MI-038 contrast to MHz above its 1 MHz range.
## Synchronous detection
162 analytical cases. Reuse MI-038 100 kHz unit-coupling impedance proxy 6.538/17.467 mOhm, multiply by assumed coupling .03/.1/.3 and peak current 20/50/100 mA. Input-referred independent white sample noise .2/1/2 mV, coherent quadrature SD sqrt(2/N)*sample noise, N=1000/10000/100000 per channel. At 50 mA, .1 coupling, 1 mV and 10000 samples: 16mm delta32.69 uV and ideal quadrature SD14.14 uV; 24mm 87.34 uV, ideal SNR6.18. This assumes known current, coherent full cycles and exact reference, ignores current-channel noise and all systematic errors. At 100 kHz, magnitude of phase error times nominal impedance <= pair contrast/3.2897 gives residual equivalent skew limit ~0.249 ns for 24mm case. This is an illustrative worst-direction total error budget if phase error were sole error; not ADC hardware clock jitter spec. At 500 kS/s aggregate two alternating channels nominal sample offset 2 us (72 degrees at 100 kHz) and only 2.5 samples/cycle/channel; deterministic delay correction and calibration essential; lower frequency or external analog I/Q before slow ADC are alternatives. Independent sample count cannot remove drift. Noise figures hypothetical and not derived from RP2040 ENOB.
