# MI-029 — Surface potential and electrochemical features
## Kelvin probe
References: https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/18/11/4068 (low-cost research Kelvin probe); https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/chimie/articles/10.1016/j.crci.2008.06.011/ (vibrating capacitor and null method); https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jpcc.4c01461 (surface chemistry and CPD). Elemental representative work functions https://pse-info.de/en/scale/work_function and https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/ema3.70019 ; not alloy-specific or invariant in air.
For an ideal parallel plate of area A, mean gap d, small vibration amplitude a, angular frequency omega, signed current amplitude approximately eps0*A*a*omega*(V_CPD-V_bias)/d² (sign depends on phase convention). 36 geometric/frequency conditions; amplitude a=.1*d. Diameter 10 mm, gap .3 mm, 100 Hz: 145.65 pA/V, 14.56 pA at 100 mV; a 1 Mohm ideal transimpedance yields 14.56 uV. Diameter 5 mm, gap .5 mm, 100 Hz gives 2.18 pA at 100 mV. No fringing, stray capacitance, amplifier current noise, leakage, mechanical/electrical feedthrough or humidity included. DC null measurement using phase-sensitive detection can cancel multiplicative gain but not offsets, trapped charge, oxide and reference work-function drift. A slow ADC alone is insufficient. Reference electrode and sample require electrical connection even though sensing face is noncontact. No empirical signal measurements.
## Electrochemical reference
https://doi.org/10.3390/met14060664 table 6: corrosion potential from polarization analysis AA6061-O -741 mV, AA2024-O -659 mV in 3.5% w/v NaCl pH 6. These are E_corr, not guaranteed instantaneous open-circuit voltage. The article reports OCP evolution over first 10 min and corrosion after testing. Surface-altering laboratory modality; no assumption that arbitrary alloys have these values or that a quick droplet test reproduces them.
## Sensitivity simulation
Two equal-prior classes, nominal difference 82 mV. 10,000 synthetic draws per class reused across 72 conditions = 1,440,000 condition-trials. Shared specimen surface shift Gaussian SD 10/25/50/100 mV, readout noise independent SD 5/20 mV, 1/4/16 repeats. Hypothetical proxy observes surface shift with independent error SD 20/50 mV, Gaussian conditional shrinkage; no identified sensor demonstrated this capability. Midpoint classifier. No empirical grade scatter separately, unknowns, kinetics, reference drift or validated joint proxy.
At 50 mV surface SD and 20 mV readout SD, 4 readings uncorrected 79.05%, proxy 20 mV 97.26%, proxy 50 mV 87.04%; 16 uncorrected 79.48%. Total residual SD for 95% equal-prior Gaussian binary classification must be below 82/3.289707=24.93 mV. At surface SD 100 mV four-readings uncorrected 66.32%, idealized 20 mV proxy 96.66%. These are conditional toy-model results, not lab accuracy.
