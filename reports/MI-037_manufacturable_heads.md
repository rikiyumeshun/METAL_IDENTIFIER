## Major correction: Kelvin bar vs surface four-point probe
Known uniform bar with end current injection gives V=I*rho*L/A. Four equally spaced collinear surface probes on homogeneous isotropic semi-infinite conductor instead give V=I*rho/(2*pi*s). Infinite thin sheet limit V=I*rho*ln(2)/(pi*t). Finite thickness and edges require correction. Sources: https://www.tek.com/en/documents/application-note/resistivity-measurements-using-model-2450-sourcemeter-smu-instrument-and-f ; https://labadviser.nanolab.dtu.dk/index.php/Specific_Process_Knowledge/Characterization/Four-Point_Probe ; https://doi.org/10.1088/0953-8984/27/22/223201 . 54 conditions (6 nominal grades, 3 spacings, 3 current levels); thin-sheet asymptote is not valid for arbitrary t/s and sample edge distances. At 5 mm pitch, 1 A, bulk Cu .549 uV, A6061 1.276 uV, A5052 1.568 uV, difference .292 uV. MI-023 MCP3425 nominal gain-8 input LSB 7.8125 uV; sub-LSB estimation may be possible with suitable dither and low noise but not demonstrated. For equal Gaussian SD, 95% pair accuracy needs total voltage SD below .292/3.2897=.089 uV, ignoring material variation. Do not assume cheap ADC directly suffices. Current rating, current source heating, thermoelectric offsets, oxidation, common mode, contact size and safety not validated. Start with known geometry bar, use surface probe only as separate R&D; avoid high current on unspecified contact probes.
## Eddy-current head tolerance
Use planar PCB coil candidate instead of hand winding; copper pattern, stackup, track and substrate lot require validation, no actual coil design or measured impedance yet. One-piece polymer carrier with integral three contact pads, coil referenced directly to carrier datum, replaceable carrier, remote screws outside calibrated sensitive zone; ensure finite sample edge distance and curvature. No adhesive thickness in the critical stack if possible. Do not assume PCB thickness/warpage and copper-to-datum variation vanish. 
Sensitivity model inherited from MI-016: A proportional exp(-2h/a); a=2/3/5 mm hypothetical, not Maxwell solution. 36 combinations of per-term half tolerance .025/.05/.1/.2 mm and 1/3/5 independent uniform stack terms. Worst positive amplitude deviation exp(2*n*tol/a)-1. Approx CV from equivalent Gaussian gap SD sqrt(n)*tol/sqrt(3), valid only as sensitivity approximation. At a=3 mm, +/- .1 mm each, five terms worst +39.56%, approx CV 8.62%; one term worst +6.89%, approx CV 3.85%. At +/- .05 mm, one term worst +3.39%; five +18.14%. These are amplitude not conductivity error or measured yield; fewer independent terms is a design objective, not achieved. For +/-5% amplitude at a=3 mm, allowable symmetric total gap error <=3/2*ln(1.05)=.0732 mm. Prefer reference calibration of static offsets, stable contact pads, wear check, tilt detection. Repositioning uncertainty remains.
