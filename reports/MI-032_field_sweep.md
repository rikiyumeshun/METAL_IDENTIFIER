# MI-032 — Distinguishing intrinsic susceptibility and magnetic impurities
## Literature and correction to interpretation of MI-031
Copper property compilation https://ncsx.pppl.gov/NCSX_Engineering/Materials/CopperProperties/PB92172766.pdf section 8, printed pp.8-32–8-35, Eq.8-9 and table 8.7: 295 K change in SI volume susceptibility = 0.00976 ppm per atomic ppm of dissolved dilute Fe for the measured high-purity Cu specimens. Pure Cu baseline about -9.65 ppm. 10 measured points transcribed; table units 1e-5 SI converted to ppm by multiplying by 10, supported by Eq.8-9. 100 atomic ppm Fe gives ~0.976 ppm change, well below the 41.57 ppm contrast in MI-031 NIST Cu specimens; do not extrapolate the dilute fit outside measured 15–304 atomic ppm or assign cause to oxygen content. Processing, contamination, precipitates and remanence may alter response. https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4608/4/6/016 studies oxidized Fe impurities. MI-031 nominal pair accuracy is not a verified grade classifier. The two large PDFs could be text-extracted but screenshot requests failed due to file size; equations and units require page-image verification before certified use.
Honda–Owen method: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/IR/nbsir79-1609.pdf printed p.210; https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.4.023093 . Under saturated impurity magnetization, apparent susceptibility = intrinsic susceptibility + M_s/H. Fit intercept in reciprocal field. This method separates only saturated magnetic contributions, not dissolved paramagnetic Fe or arbitrary contamination.
## Simulation
10,000 synthetic Cu specimens reused across 36 conditions = 360,000 condition-trials. Intrinsic chi=-9.65+N(0,.5²) ppm; impurity amplitude c uniform 0–16 ppm*T is an assumed stress range, NOT an empirical concentration. Applied B .1/.2/.3/.4 T, known volume 1 cm³, gradient B² scaling with 20 T²/m at .4 T (hypothetical fixture). Force response in equivalent mg: k(B)*(chi-.36+c*tanh(B/Bs)/B), where Bs=0 means already saturated and k is MI-031 force coefficient; independent force noise SD .1/.5/1 mg. Fit one high-field apparent value, two-field or four-field weighted linear regression in force space (saturated model). No geometry or field-map error, hysteresis, blank drift, nonuniform magnetization, or empirical specimens.
At .5 mg force SD, saturated case median absolute intrinsic chi error: one field 20.02 ppm, two 1.91, four 1.47. Four-field lack-of-fit flag (chi-square residual >5.991, two df) 5.18%. At assumed Bs=.15 T, four-field median error 6.47 ppm and flag 42.56%; at Bs=.3 T, error 12.17 and flag 22.93%. Model mismatch can pass residual check; do not infer purity or grade from corrected value alone. Two points cannot test fit. At .1 mg force SD saturated four-field median error .294 ppm.
